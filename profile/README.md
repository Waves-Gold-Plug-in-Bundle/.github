## Plugin Categories

### Equalizers and Filters

Equalizers help shape the frequency content of vocals, instruments, and other audio sources. They can reduce masking, manage resonances, and improve tonal balance within a mix.

### Compressors and Dynamics

Dynamics processors help control level variations and shape the response of individual tracks. Depending on the selected plugin, available functions may include compression, expansion, gating, and limiting.

### Vocal Processing

Vocal tools can help refine presence, control dynamics, and add character to recorded performances. The appropriate processing chain depends on the singer, recording conditions, and production style.

### Reverb and Delay

Time-based effects add ambience, depth, echoes, and spatial movement. They can be used subtly to place a sound in the mix or creatively to produce more noticeable effects.

### Stereo Imaging and Enhancement

Stereo processors help adjust the spatial presentation of suitable audio material. Use these tools carefully and check the mix in mono when stereo compatibility matters.

### Distortion and Sound Design

Creative effects can introduce harmonic content, stronger coloration, and more distinctive textures. Their role can range from subtle character enhancement to deliberately prominent effects.

### Metering and Analysis

Monitoring tools help assess signal levels and other audio characteristics during mixing. They complement critical listening and help identify potential balance or level issues.

## Typical Mixing Workflow

1. **Prepare the session.** Organize tracks and check recording levels before adding processors.
2. **Correct tonal balance.** Use equalization to address frequency conflicts and unwanted resonances.
3. **Control dynamics.** Apply compression or other suitable dynamics processing where needed.
4. **Shape the space.** Add reverb and delay to support the arrangement and create depth.
5. **Refine individual sources.** Use vocal, stereo, or creative effects where they serve a clear purpose.
6. **Review the mix.** Listen across the full arrangement and check for excessive processing, masking, or inconsistent levels.

These stages are flexible. Not every track needs every type of processor, and a simpler chain may produce a clearer result.
